## 📌 Overview
**HostelHub** is a web-based Hostel Visitor Management Portal designed to replace manual paper registers with a centralized, organized digital workflow. Hostel administrators and reception staff can register visitors, monitor real-time occupancy, search and filter logs, and record checkouts in one place.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Home & Quick Navigation:** Clean landing portal introducing visitor registration, organized records, and quick search.
- **Live Dashboard Metrics:** Automatically updates counts for **Total Visitors**, **Today's Visits**, **Currently Inside**, and **Checked Out**, along with a **Recent Visitors** feed and **Visitor Overview**.
- **Visitor Registration:** Structured form to capture visitor name, contact number, visit purpose (*Parent Visit, Student Visit, Meeting, Delivery, Personal, Other*), person to meet, room number, date, and time in.
- **Visitor Records & Filtering:** Full tabular log displaying visitor details, entry/exit timestamps, status (*Inside* / *Exited*), and checkout actions, with search and filter options by purpose and status.
